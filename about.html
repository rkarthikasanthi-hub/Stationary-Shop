About SVM

Rooted in Tamil Nadu tradition, empowering students and professionals since 2016.

Our Story

SVM started as a humble local shop in South India with a clear goal: to provide affordable and premium quality stationery products directly to students, families, and working professionals.

Deeply rooted in our local community, we understand the exact study requirements of students and the organizational needs of offices. Through transparent pricing, genuine product trust, and warm customer service, we have grown into a household name for stationery shopping.

Our Mission

"To make quality stationery easily accessible and affordable for everyone, encouraging creativity, learning, and productivity in every classroom and workplace."

Our Vision

"To become the most trusted and comprehensive stationery destination across South India, celebrated for customer dedication, varied products, and ethical retailing."
